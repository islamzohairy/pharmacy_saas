Part 1: Egyptian Pharmacy Market Landscape

Part 1 — Egyptian Pharmacy Market Landscape
Market Understanding for Building Low-Cost Pharmacy Management SaaS in Egypt
Research objective: Understand whether Egypt has a large enough, underserved pharmacy software market and identify the ideal customer segment for a low-cost SaaS product.
Confidence level:
	•	Market size: Medium confidence (public data is fragmented)
	•	Technology adoption: Medium-low confidence (requires direct interviews for validation)
	•	Operational behavior: Medium confidence (based on industry patterns, software reviews, and regional pharmacy practices)
	•	Product opportunity: Strategic hypothesis requiring validation

1. Executive Summary
The opportunity
Egypt represents a potentially attractive market for pharmacy management software because:
	•	It has a very large number of community pharmacies.
	•	The majority are independent businesses, not sophisticated chains.
	•	Many pharmacies operate with basic tools:
	•	Excel
	•	Paper notebooks
	•	Simple cashier systems
	•	Local desktop software
	•	Large ERP systems are often too expensive or complex.
	•	Owners increasingly need:
	•	Inventory visibility
	•	Expiry control
	•	Profit understanding
	•	Employee monitoring
	•	Remote access
The biggest opportunity is not replacing enterprise pharmacy ERP systems.
The opportunity is:
Build the "Shopify for small Egyptian pharmacies": simple, affordable, mobile-first, easy onboarding, focused on daily business survival.

2. Size of Egyptian Pharmacy Market
Estimated number of pharmacies
Public sources vary, but available government data indicates Egypt has approximately:
	•	70,000+ public pharmacies
	•	Around 79,000 registered working pharmacists
	•	More than 71,000 public pharmacies listed in Ministry of Health statistics (depending on classification and year). IDSC
The Egyptian market is therefore one of the largest pharmacy retail markets in the Middle East.
However:
The number of software-buying customers is much smaller.
A realistic SaaS target is not 70,000 pharmacies.
A better estimate:
Segment	Approximate opportunity
Large chains	<100 companies
Medium chains	Hundreds
Professional independent pharmacies	10,000–20,000
Small traditional pharmacies	40,000+
The best initial SaaS market:
Independent pharmacies with enough revenue pain to pay monthly.

1. Inventory Problems
Why inventory is the #1 opportunity
A pharmacy is fundamentally an inventory business.
The owner is managing:
	•	thousands of products
	•	different suppliers
	•	different prices
	•	different batches
	•	expiration dates
	•	availability pressure
Small mistakes directly become financial losses.

Problem 1: Expired Medicine Losses
Who suffers?
	•	Pharmacy owner
	•	Pharmacist manager
Current situation
Many pharmacies discover expired medicines:
	•	during shelf checking
	•	during inspection preparation
	•	when attempting to return products

Current workaround
Manual methods:
	•	periodic shelf checking
	•	employee memory
	•	handwritten notes

Why it fails
Because humans cannot reliably track:
	•	thousands of expiry dates
	•	slow-moving products
	•	seasonal medicines

Business impact
Example:
A pharmacy has:
10,000 medicine units
Even 1% expiration:
Could represent thousands of EGP lost annually.

Software opportunity
High-value feature:
Expiry Intelligence
	•	expiry countdown
	•	alerts:
	•	6 months
	•	3 months
	•	1 month
	•	slow-moving inventory detection
	•	suggested actions:
	•	discount
	•	return
	•	stop ordering
